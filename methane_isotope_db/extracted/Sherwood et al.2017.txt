## Key insights:
- Coal gas mean δ¹³C (−49.5‰) is MUCH more depleted than −35 to −37‰ assumed in previous budgets
- Coal gas mean δ²H (−232‰) is 60–120‰ lighter than literature values
- Natural gas mean δ²H (−194‰) is 10–15‰ lighter than literature values
- Shale gas: lower mean δ¹³C (−42.5‰) and δ²H (−167‰) than previously assumed
- Flux-weighted atmospheric mean δ¹³C ≈ −53.6‰ (inferred from atmospheric measurements)
- Flux-weighted atmospheric δ²H ranges between −245 and −415‰
- Ruminant values NOT weighted by C3 vs C4 diet proportion
- Biomass burning values NOT weighted by C3 vs C4 vegetation proportion

## Notes on representativeness:
- δ¹³C data representative of 84% of global gas production, 80% of coal production
- δ²H data more sparse: only 2,878 fossil fuel samples vs 8,128 for δ¹³C
- Non-fossil δ²H extremely sparse: only 4 biomass burning, 1 termite sample
