## Key δ¹³C values (Keeling plot / source signatures):

### Tropical Wetlands:
- δ¹³C = −61.5 ± 2.9‰ to −53.0 ± 0.4‰ (this study, multiple sites)
- Literature range: −66 to −52‰ (Table 1)
- Freshwater mangrove swamps: −77.7 ± 0.2‰ to −70.1 ± 2.4‰
- Brackish/marine mangroves: −54.6 ± 0.7‰
- European Arctic wetlands (Fisher 2017): −71 ± 1‰
- Hydrogenotrophic methanogenesis: −110 to −60‰
- Acetotrophic methanogenesis: −60 to −50‰

### Rice paddies:
- Hong Kong rice: −58.7 ± 0.4‰ and −58.9 ± 0.4‰
- Tropical range (Table 1): −70 to −51‰
- Japanese rice paddies (Tyler 1994): −70‰ (seasonal)
- Temperate rice (Tyler 2002): −63 ± 5‰
- General rice (Dlugokencky 2011): −62 ± 3‰
- General rice (Schaefer 2016): −59 to −65‰

### Ruminants:
- Wild African buffalo (C3 diet): −63.3 ± 0.4‰
- Domesticated Hong Kong cows (C3): −70.5 ± 0.7‰
- Farmed cattle Zimbabwe (C4 diet): −56.9 ± 0.4‰ to −52.5 ± 0.6‰
- C3-fed ruminants (literature): −74 to −60‰
- C4-fed ruminants (literature): −55 to −50‰

### Biomass burning:
- C3 plants (Hong Kong): −33.4 ± 0.6‰ and −28.5 ± 0.4‰
- African woodlands: −30‰
- C4 plants (Zimbabwe): −18.7 ± 0.3‰ and −15.9 ± 1.3‰
- Zambian savanna burns: −16.6‰ (flaming stage)
- Agricultural grass/dried branches: −24 to −32‰

### Global budget context:
- Global average source signature: −53.6 to −53.4‰
- OH kinetic isotope effect (KIE): 4 to 6‰
- Cl sink enrichment: 2.6 ± 1.2‰
- Background atmospheric δ¹³C-CH₄: −47.4 to −47.2‰
- Sink fractionation leaves residual ambient at ~−47‰
